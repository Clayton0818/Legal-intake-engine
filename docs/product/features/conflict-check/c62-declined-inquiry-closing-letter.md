# c62 — Declined and conflicted-out inquiries: neutral closing letter, record kept

**Board card:** `c62` — Conflict-check engine · Declined and conflicted-out inquiries: neutral closing letter, record kept
**Priority:** P1
**Status:** Product spec (draft). Docs only; no code. Letter wording and retention are compliance-sensitive and flagged for licensed Texas attorney review.
**Existing spec on main:** none for this card. Builds on the intake flow's terminal states (`docs/product/spec/`, `c12`: "declined for conflict", "not eligible", "out of scope", "referred out") and `c2`'s retention schedule.

---

## 1. Summary

When intake stops because of a conflict, or the firm declines a prospective client for any reason, the prospect receives a neutral non-engagement letter generated by the Document engine: the firm cannot take the matter, no attorney-client relationship was formed, time limits may apply so they should consult another lawyer promptly, and (optionally) a referral. The letter never says there is a conflict or with whom. The prospect's and other parties' names stay in the party index (`c56`) so future checks catch them; case details are kept to the minimum and handled under the retention policy (`c2`).

## 2. Users and problem

**Users**
- **Prospective client** who is not taken on.
- **Conflicts attorney / reviewing lawyer:** approves the letter before it goes (or approves a template for auto-send, see §4.2).
- **Intake staff:** sees that a letter went out and when.

**Problem.** Without a clear written non-engagement, a prospect may believe the firm is representing them and let deadlines pass, which is a malpractice and client-harm risk. A careless letter can also reveal another client's confidential information (e.g. "we represent your spouse"). And the prospect's record is needed for future conflict checks, while their case details should not be kept longer than necessary.

## 3. Scope

**In scope**
- Letter generation from a firm-approved template (`c85`) for these terminal outcomes: declined for conflict (`c59` decision `declined`, or `c3` `definite`), declined by firm choice, not eligible, out of scope, and did-not-hire after a consultation (firm setting).
- Required letter elements (§5 rule 2).
- Optional referral (to a bar lawyer referral service or firm-chosen list).
- Delivery by a verified, DV-safe channel (`c51`, `c71`, `c103`), with delivery tracking.
- Keeping names in the index; minimising and scheduling deletion of narrative.

**Out of scope**
- Deciding to decline (`c59`, firm intake rules `c73`).
- Closing letters for retained clients at matter end (`c90`).
- Refunds of any consultation fee (Billing & trust; see flag in §9).
- Abandoned intakes where the person never gave contact details (nothing to send to).

## 4. Behaviour

### 4.1 Trigger
1. Intake reaches a terminal state of declined for conflict, not eligible, out of scope, declined by firm choice, or (if enabled) did-not-hire after consult.
2. The system creates a "send non-engagement letter" task due within a firm-set window (default 1 business day), owned by the reviewing lawyer.

### 4.2 Drafting and approval
1. The Document engine fills the firm's approved non-engagement template with the prospect's name, date of contact and optional referral. The template has **no** field for conflict reason, other parties or case facts, so they cannot leak in.
2. **Conflict declines always need a lawyer to approve the individual letter** before sending. For other decline types, the firm may approve the template once and allow auto-send (firm setting, default: lawyer approves every letter).
3. The AI may pick a referral from the firm's configured list based on practice area; it must not add legal commentary, deadline estimates, or advice.

### 4.3 Sending
1. Letter is delivered through the prospect's verified channel: portal (if one was created), secure link by email to the confirmed address, or postal mail (firm staff prints).
2. For Family Law / DV-flagged intakes, only the safe contact method the prospect chose is used; nothing goes to a shared address or device.
3. The letter is stored as a document against the inquiry, with sent / delivered / bounced status logged (`c6`).
4. **Bounce or no valid contact:** flagged internally to the reviewing lawyer (`c51` bounce rule) with a task to try another channel or record that no channel exists.

### 4.4 Record keeping
1. Parties remain in `c56` with inquiry link, role, contact date, and which firm people spoke with the prospect (needed for a future Rule 1.18 / screening analysis).
2. Narrative and case details follow `c2` (default 90 days for non-engaged inquiries), then are deleted or de-identified by a scheduled job, unless a legal hold is set.
3. The letter itself and its delivery record are kept with the conflicts record (open question 2).

### 4.5 Failure paths
- Template not yet approved by a firm lawyer: letters cannot be generated; the firm admin sees a setup flag and the task remains open.
- Prospect replies to the letter: the reply files to the inquiry and goes to the reviewing lawyer; the AI replies only with a neutral acknowledgement, never re-opens intake on its own.
- Prospect re-contacts the firm later: a new intake runs a fresh conflict check that will find the earlier inquiry.

## 5. Business rules

1. Every declined or conflicted-out inquiry with a known contact channel gets a written non-engagement letter. **Firm-configurable:** whether did-not-hire-after-consult also gets one, default on.
2. Required letter elements (wording set by the firm's attorney-reviewed template): the firm will not represent them in the matter; no attorney-client relationship was formed; time limits may apply to their matter and they should promptly consult another lawyer; the firm has not given an opinion on the merits.
3. The letter never states that a conflict exists, never names any other person or matter, and never states or estimates a deadline.
4. Conflict-decline letters require individual lawyer approval. **Firm-configurable** for other decline types, default: individual approval.
5. Send window: **firm-configurable**, default 1 business day after the terminal state, counted in firm business hours; the task goes overdue via `c45`.
6. Referral block: **firm-configurable**, default include the firm's chosen referral source; can be off.
7. Delivery uses only verified and, where flagged, DV-safe channels.
8. Names and inquiry links stay in the party index; narrative is minimised per `c2`.
9. All actions logged in `c6`.

## 6. Data model touchpoints

**Reuse:** `intake_sessions.terminal_state`, `matters.stage` (`declined_conflict`, `did_not_hire_referred_out`), `documents` (letter), `outbox` (delivery), `scheduled_tasks` (send window, retention purge), `intake_events` (audit), `matters.retention_years` / `eligible_for_deletion_at` for retention timing where a prospective matter exists.

**Proposed**
- **`non_engagement_letters`**: `id`, `tenant_id`, `intake_session_id`, `matter_id` (nullable), `decline_type`, `template_version_id`, `document_id`, `approved_by_user_id`, `approved_at`, `channel`, `sent_at`, `delivered_at`, `bounced_at`, `referral_included boolean`.
- `firm_config_versions.config`: `non_engagement.template_id`, `auto_send_types`, `send_window_business_days`, `referral_sources`.
- Scheduled task type `inquiry_narrative_purge` implementing `c2`'s 90-day default.

## 7. Notifications and visibility

- **Prospect:** receives only the letter (portal or secure link; email body minimal: "A letter from [Firm] is available", link). No firm-side flags, ever.
- **Reviewing lawyer:** approval task, overdue flags (`c45`) and minimal email (`c51`).
- **Intake staff:** sees "letter sent / delivered / bounced" status; not the conflict reason unless conflicts role.
- **Conflicts role:** sees the letter linked to the conflict decision in `c63`.

## 8. Dependencies

**Needs first:** `c59` (decline decision), `c3`, `c85` (templates), `c84` (document store), `c51` (email and bounce handling), `c71` (verified contact), `c45` (task timer), `c2` (retention; currently waiting on attorney review), `c56`.
**Feeds:** `c63` (log), `c56` (records kept), `c74` (lead reporting counts declines).

## 9. Compliance and review flags

**Texas attorney must review:**
1. The full letter wording, including the "time limits may apply" statement and the referral block, so that it neither gives legal advice nor omits a warning a Texas lawyer would consider necessary. (Card note: "Wording needs attorney review.")
2. That the letter reveals nothing protected by Rule 1.05 about another client.
3. Whether a letter is required or advisable for every decline type, and for did-not-hire after a consult.
4. Minimum record for Rule 1.18 purposes vs. `c2`'s 90-day narrative deletion.
5. Referral sources: any referral arrangement needs checking against Texas referral rules already covered in `c1` (§5, Occupations Code ch. 952); the product should only offer referral lists the firm chooses.

**CPA (and attorney) review:**
6. If a paid consultation fee was collected (`c67`) and the firm then declines for conflict before the consult happens, how the fee is refunded and from which account. This card does not specify refunds; flagged so `c82` covers it.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the conflicts attorney records "declined", **when** saved, **then** a non-engagement letter draft and an approval task due in 1 business day are created for the reviewing lawyer.
2. **Given** a draft for a conflict decline, **when** the lawyer views it, **then** it contains no other party names, no mention of a conflict, and no deadline estimate, and the template has no fields that could hold them.
3. **Given** a DV-flagged Family Law intake with a safe email chosen, **when** the letter is sent, **then** it goes only to that address with a minimal email body and a secure link.
4. **Given** the email bounces, **when** the bounce is received, **then** the reviewing lawyer gets an internal flag and a task to use another channel.
5. **Given** a declined inquiry, **when** 90 days pass without a legal hold, **then** narrative fields are deleted or de-identified while the party names and inquiry link remain in `c56`.
6. **Given** a later intake names the declined prospect, **when** the check runs, **then** the earlier inquiry is found.
7. **Given** no firm-approved template exists, **when** a decline happens, **then** no letter is generated and the firm admin sees a setup flag.
