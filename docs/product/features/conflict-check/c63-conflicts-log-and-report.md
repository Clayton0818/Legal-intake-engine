# c63 — Conflicts log and report for the firm

**Board card:** `c63` — Conflict-check engine · Conflicts log and report for the firm
**Priority:** P1
**Status:** Product spec (draft). Docs only; no code. Export content is flagged for licensed Texas attorney review.
**Existing spec on main:** none for this card. Builds on the audit trail design (`c6`, `docs/architecture/audit-log-compliance-trail.md`) and `conflict_check_results` (`c19`).

---

## 1. Summary

An internal, searchable log with one record per conflict check: who ran it (or what triggered it), which parties were searched, what hit, the attorney's decision and reason, waivers with signature dates, and screens with notice dates. It shows the open queue of `possible` conflicts waiting on a decision and how long each has waited, and exports for malpractice-insurer applications and bar inquiries. Access is limited to the conflicts role and the firm owner/admin.

## 2. Users and problem

**Users**
- **Conflicts attorney:** works the open queue; looks up history.
- **Firm owner/admin:** oversight, insurer applications, bar inquiries.
- **Malpractice insurer / bar** (indirect): receive exports prepared by the firm.

**Problem.** Malpractice applications commonly ask how a firm checks conflicts and may ask for evidence; a bar grievance may require the firm to show what it checked and decided. The raw audit events (`intake_events`) exist, but a firm needs a readable per-check record and a queue view, not rows in a table.

## 3. Scope

**In scope**
- A per-check record view assembled from `c3`/`c58` results, `c57` match reasons, `c59` decisions and waivers, `c60` screens and notices, `c61` lateral checks, `c62` letters, `c97` interest hits (restricted further, see rule 4).
- Search and filters: party name (any variant), date range, trigger, outcome, decision, decider, matter, lawyer screened, open/closed.
- Open-queue dashboard: open `possible`/`definite` checks with age in business hours, due time and overdue status.
- Summary stats: checks run, hit rate, decisions by type, median time to decision, overdue count.
- Export to PDF and CSV of a single check, a filtered set, or a summary.
- Tile in the cross-engine reports (`c101`), "conflict checks pending".

**Out of scope**
- Creating or changing decisions (done in `c59`; the log is read-only).
- Writing insurer application answers.
- Any client-facing view.

## 4. Behaviour

### 4.1 Record assembly
1. Each `conflict_check_results` row is one log record.
2. The record shows: trigger (intake, party added, reopened, lateral, periodic, interest), who started it (user or system), timestamp, parties searched (names as entered), hits with match reasons and strength, outcome, each decision (who, when, reason, rule-table reference, overrides), waivers (client, sent, signed, countersigned dates), screens (who is screened, start date, notice task and notice sent date), letters (`c62`), and the gate open/closed history.
3. The log reads from the source tables; it does not keep a second editable copy. Corrections show as superseding decisions, never edits.

### 4.2 Open queue
1. Lists open checks, oldest first, with waiting time in firm business hours and the real-clock age, due time, assignee, and overdue badge (from `c45`).
2. Clicking opens the `c59` decision screen (if the user has the conflicts role).

### 4.3 Export
1. User picks scope (one check, a filter, or summary) and format (PDF, CSV).
2. Before export, the user chooses a redaction level: **full** (for internal use or a bar inquiry, as the firm decides) or **summary** (counts and process only, no party names; default for insurer applications).
3. The export records who exported what, when, with which filter and redaction level, in `c6`.
4. Exports are generated by the worker and available by a time-limited link (default 24 hours) to the requester only.

### 4.4 Edge cases and failure paths
- Very large exports: run as background jobs with progress; failures flag the requester.
- Party names merged later (`c56`): the log shows the name as entered at check time and the current merged record.
- Parties or narrative deleted under `c2`: the log keeps what the conflicts retention rule keeps (names, dates, decisions) and shows "details removed under retention policy" where narrative was.
- Screened user is also firm admin: they cannot see log records for matters they are screened from (`c60` wins over role).

## 5. Business rules

1. Access: conflicts role and firm owner/admin only. All other roles, including lawyers on the matter, cannot view the log.
2. The log is read-only. No user can edit or delete a log record.
3. Screens apply: a user screened from a matter cannot see that matter's log records, whatever their role.
4. `c97` lawyer-interest hits are visible only to the conflicts role, not to firm owner/admin (unless they also hold the conflicts role). **Firm-configurable:** no.
5. Every view of a full record and every export is logged in `c6`.
6. Default export redaction for insurer applications is "summary" (no party names). **Firm-configurable** default.
7. Waiting time on the open queue is shown in firm business hours (matching the `c59` timer) and real-clock age.
8. Export links expire after 24 hours (**firm-configurable**, max 7 days).
9. Log retention is at least as long as the conflicts records it shows and never shorter than the matter's retention (`c6` §4).

## 6. Data model touchpoints

**Reuse:** `conflict_check_results`, `intake_events` (audit source), `matters`, `users`, `documents` (waivers, letters, exports).
**Reads proposed tables from sibling specs:** `conflict_decisions`, `conflict_waivers`, `conflict_gates` (`c59`); `ethical_screens` and notice tasks (`c60`, proposed there); `lateral_checks` (`c61`); `non_engagement_letters` (`c62`); `interest_disclosure_hits` (`c97`).
**Proposed**
- **`conflict_log_view`**: a database view (or read model refreshed by the worker) joining the above, tenant-scoped via RLS, plus a role-based policy.
- **`conflict_exports`**: `id`, `tenant_id`, `requested_by_user_id`, `filter jsonb`, `format`, `redaction_level`, `document_id`, `expires_at`, `created_at`.
- `conflict_check_results`: add `trigger`, `triggered_by_user_id`, `matter_id`, nullable `intake_session_id` (shared change with `c58`/`c59`).

## 7. Notifications and visibility

- Internal only. No client or prospect ever sees any part of the log.
- Overdue decisions already flag via `c59`/`c45`; the log shows them but sends no extra flags.
- Optional weekly digest to firm owner/admin: counts only (open, overdue, decided this week), no names (**firm-configurable**, default off). Uses `c51` delivery.
- Export completed / failed: in-app notice to the requester only.

## 8. Dependencies

**Needs first:** `c3`, `c58`, `c59`, `c60`, `c6` (shipped), `c34`/`c99` (roles).
**Uses if present:** `c61`, `c62`, `c97`.
**Feeds:** `c101` (reports), firm insurer applications and bar responses.

## 9. Compliance and review flags (licensed Texas attorney)

1. **Export contents:** what may be included in an export sent outside the firm (insurer, bar) without breaching Rule 1.05, and whether "summary" redaction is sufficient for insurer applications.
2. **Retention of the log**, tied to `c6` §4 and `c2`, including for declined prospects.
3. **Owner/admin access** to full conflict records (including other clients' names and decisions) when the owner is not the conflicts attorney.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a check with two hits, a consent decision and two signed waivers, **when** the conflicts attorney opens its log record, **then** all hits with reasons, the decision with who/when/why and both signature dates are shown.
2. **Given** a user with the `attorney` role but not the conflicts role, **when** they request the log, **then** access is denied and the attempt is logged.
3. **Given** three open `possible` checks, **when** the owner opens the queue, **then** they are listed oldest first with waiting time in business hours and overdue badges.
4. **Given** a filtered export with "summary" redaction, **when** generated, **then** the file contains no party names, and the export is recorded in the audit trail with the filter and redaction level.
5. **Given** a firm admin screened from matter M, **when** they search the log, **then** records for matter M are not returned.
6. **Given** any log record, **when** a user tries to edit or delete it via the API, **then** the request is refused.
7. **Given** a `c97` interest hit, **when** a firm owner without the conflicts role views the log, **then** the hit is not shown.
