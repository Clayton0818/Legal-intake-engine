# docs/

Compliance research, specs, and architecture decision records for the Legal Intake Engine.

Each subfolder corresponds to a track from the [Legal Intake Build](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c002dcf5-570b-4d92-9afd-9cde9d8daa3e) board:

- `compliance/` — UPL (unauthorized practice of law) research by state, data privacy and retention policy, terms of service drafts.
- `product/` — conversational intake flow design, feature specs, UX decisions.
- `architecture/` — architecture decision records (ADRs) for multi-tenant design, integrations, and infrastructure choices.
- `security/` — encryption, access control, and audit-log design.
- `go-to-market/` — competitive research, pricing models, pilot firm planning.

A doc lands here *before* the card it belongs to moves out of Scoping/In Progress on the board. If there's no doc, the work isn't actually done yet, regardless of what the board says.
